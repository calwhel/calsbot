📋 ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES TO COPY TO RAILWAY
================================================

Copy these from your Replit Secrets and paste into Railway Variables:

TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN=
BROADCAST_CHAT_ID=
CRYPTOPANIC_API_KEY=
ENCRYPTION_KEY=
EXCHANGE=
SYMBOLS=
SESSION_SECRET=

# X / Twitter auto-poster (Bitunix signup drive) — required to resume posting
TWITTER_ENABLED=1
TWITTER_CONSUMER_KEY=
TWITTER_CONSUMER_SECRET=
TWITTER_ACCESS_TOKEN=
TWITTER_ACCESS_TOKEN_SECRET=
TWITTER_BEARER_TOKEN=
BITUNIX_REFERRAL_URL=https://www.bitunix.com/register?vipCode=tradehubsave
TWITTER_AUTO_REPLY_ENABLED=1
# TWITTER_BEARER_TOKEN required for mover auto-replies (search API)

Note: Database variables (DATABASE_URL, PGHOST, etc.) will be auto-created by Railway when you add PostgreSQL.

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Make sure to set Railway region to EU (Ireland or Frankfurt) BEFORE deploying!
